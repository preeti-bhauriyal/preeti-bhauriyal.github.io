About Me

Preeti Bhauriyal is a Postdoctoral Fellow and Subgroup Leader at Technische Universität Dresden, working in the group of Prof. Thomas Heine, specializing in the computational design of organic 2D crystals (O2DCs) and inorganic materials for applications in batteries, ion transport, and photocatalysis.

Supported by a prestigious Humboldt Research Fellowship (2021–2023) and a Maria Reiche Postdoctoral Fellowship (2024–2025), she now leads a subgroup on heavy-ion transport in O2DCs. Her work bridges high-level theory with experimental collaboration, producing 34+ peer-reviewed publications (h-index 23, 1922+ citations) in journals including Nature Communications, Advanced Materials, and JACS.

My Research

My research interest lies in rational design of organic 2D crystals (O2DCs) — COFs and MOFs — using computational and machine learning techniques. I focus on engineering structural and electronic properties for next-generation batteries, photocatalysis, and selective ion transport membranes.

⚡
Conversion Electrochemistry of O2DCs for Metal-Sulfur Batteries

Tuning redox-active functionalities in covalent and metallic O2DCs to overcome polysulfide shuttling, sluggish kinetics, and poor conductivity — in close collaboration with experimental groups.

🌊
Charged 2D Membranes for Selective Ion Transport

Ultrathin positively charged 2D organic membranes regulating ion dynamics and osmotic energy conversion; demonstrated high cation selectivity in crystalline imine-based 2D polymer membranes.

☀️
Photocatalytic Water Splitting in O2DCs

Topology and donor–acceptor guided design of thiophene-boron 2D conjugated polymers; evaluating pore engineering, band edges, and carrier dynamics for H₂ evolution.

🤖
ML-Driven Screening of O2DC Databases

Machine learning classification of experimentally synthesised O2DC databases for anode/cathode identification in next-generation metal-ion batteries, targeting voltage and theoretical capacity.
